# 06: Wish List

**Target runtime:** ~3:00
**Goal:** Show that the Wish List is more than a single list, it's multiple color-coded lists, with TE import, link-based adds, and a running spend estimate.

---

| VISUAL | VOICEOVER |
|---|---|

| **[0:12–0:25]** Cut to MCS sidebar → click **Wish List**. The view opens. Two-tier header visible: action buttons on top, tab strip below. | "The Wish List view in My Crafty Stash gives every release, every shop, every someday-pile its own home." |

| **[0:45–1:00]** New tab appears in the strip with a colored dot, name, and "(0)" badge. ## Shot list reminders

- For the **Add item from link** demo, pick a real shop URL that returns clean metadata: TE's own product pages are ideal.
- The TE import demo needs an actual exported wishlist file. If none exists, skip the import section and lean harder on the link-add demo.
- The colored tab dots are the visual signature of this feature. Make sure they're vivid and distinct on screen.
