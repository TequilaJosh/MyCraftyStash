# 04: Card Build Wizard

**Target runtime:** ~3:30
**Goal:** Demystify the wizard, show that you walk a card section by section, each pill button becoming "Done!" as you go, until you've documented the entire build.

---

| VISUAL | VOICEOVER |
|---|---|
| **[0:00–0:12]** Cold open: a finished card on the desk. Voice asks (text on screen): *"Quick: what cardstock did you use?"* Pause. *"What sentiment stamp?"* Pause. *"What die?"* | "If you can't answer those three questions about a card you made last month… the Card Build Wizard is about to become your favorite feature." |
| **[0:12–0:25]** Cut to a project in Edit mode. Click the **Card Builder** button. The Card Build Wizard window opens: title bar reads **"Start Project."** | "Inside any project, the **Card Builder** button launches the wizard. It opens in its own window so you can drag it next to your card." |
| **[0:25–0:45]** Wide shot of the wizard. Center grid of pill buttons: **Cardbase, Cardstock, Background Mat, Focal Image, Sentiment, Decoration, Details, Inside of Card, Cardfold, Adhesives, How was it cut, Additional Mat, Embellishments, Focal Mat.** | "The wizard is laid out as a grid of the parts of a card. Cardbase, cardstock, focal image, sentiment, embellishments: fourteen sections in all. You don't have to use every one. You only fill in what your card has." |
| **[0:45–1:05]** Click **Cardbase**. Side panel opens. Combobox dropdown shows inventory items (thumbnail + name). Pick one. Type a description or quantity. | "Click any section and a side panel slides open. The dropdown is your live inventory: every cardstock you own, with a thumbnail. Pick the one you used, add a quick note like '4¼ by 5½, scored at 5½'." |
| **[1:05–1:20]** Click the small **✕** next to an added item to demonstrate removal. Re-add. Close the side panel. The **Cardbase** pill is now crimson with white text reading **"Cardbase - Done!"** | "Hit close and the pill turns red: **Done!** It's a visual checklist. At a glance you can see what you've documented and what's still open." |
| **[1:20–1:50]** Click **Cardstock**. Pick a colored cardstock. Click **Focal Image**. Pick a stamp from inventory. Click **Sentiment**. Pick a sentiment stamp. Each pill turns "Done!" in sequence: montage with subtle satisfying click sound. | "Walk the card section by section. Cardstock. Focal image. Sentiment. Each pick pulls from your real inventory: so the supplies on the card are always tied to actual items you own." |
| **[1:50–2:10]** Click **Embellishments**. Add two items (sequins, ribbon). Note: the side panel handles multiple items per section. | "Some sections take more than one supply. Embellishments, for example: add as many as your card has, each one with its own note." |
| **[2:10–2:35]** Cut to a wide shot showing most pills now in the "Done!" state. The remaining grey pills are clearly the optional ones. Hover **Adhesives** → side panel opens. | "Adhesives, additional mats, the inside of the card, even how you cut it: every detail you want to remember has a slot. The optional sections stay grey. They're not failures, just things this particular card didn't need." |
| **[2:35–2:55]** Click the **Create Card** button at the bottom. Wizard closes. Cut to the project's **How It Was Made** card: every section we filled is now listed with the linked items. | "When you're done, hit **Create Card**. The wizard closes and your project's **How It Was Made** card is now a complete recipe. Anyone who opens this project, including future-you: can recreate it exactly." |
| **[2:55–3:20]** Back on the project. Click **I Made One!** → tick "Subtract materials from inventory." Stock counts visibly tick down on the items used. | "And because the supplies are real inventory items: when you remake the card later, the **I Made One!** button can deduct them straight from your stock." |
| **[3:20–3:30]** End card: "Next up: **Color Match**." | "Next, let's talk about colors." |

## Shot list reminders

- The transition from grey pill → red "Done!" pill is the visual hook. Cut tight to it once or twice.
- Pre-stock inventory with photogenic items: a crisp cardstock, a recognizable stamp set, sequins.
- Don't fill every single section in the demo: leaving 2–3 grey pills shows the "use what you need" message.
