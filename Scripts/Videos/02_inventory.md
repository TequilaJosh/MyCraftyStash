# 02: Inventory

**Target runtime:** ~3:30
**Goal:** Show how a crafter searches, adds, and enriches an item, including stock, sentiments, and purchase history.

---

| VISUAL | VOICEOVER |
|---|---|
| **[0:00–0:10]** B-roll: hands flipping through stamp sets in a drawer, then giving up. | "You bought a stamp set last spring. The right one. You just… can't find it." |
| **[0:10–0:20]** Cut to MCS Inventory view. The card grid loads: dozens of items with photo, name, theme, stock badge. | "The Inventory view is the heart of My Crafty Stash. Every supply you own, photographed, categorized, and searchable." |
| **[0:20–0:40]** Type "thank you" in the search box → grid filters live. Toggle the **Sentiments only** checkbox → results narrow further. | "Searching is instant. Type any word, a name, a theme, a sentiment: and the grid filters as you go. Need to narrow down? Use the **Name only**, **Theme only**, or **Discontinued only** toggles to tell it where to look." |
| **[0:40–0:55]** Open the **Type** dropdown → pick "Stamp." Subtype checkboxes appear: tick "Sentiment Set." | "Use the **Type** dropdown to filter by what kind of supply you're after. When you pick a type with subtypes, like Stamps, the matching subtype filters appear right underneath." |
| **[0:55–1:10]** Click "+ Add Item" → Add Item dialog opens. Cursor walks the fields: Name, Type, Theme, Item Number, Price, Date Purchased, Image. | "Adding a new item takes about thirty seconds. Drop in a photo, give it a name, pick a type, and fill in the details you care about. Everything except the name is optional." |
| **[1:10–1:25]** Click Save → the new item appears in the grid. Click its card → Item Detail view opens. | "Your item lands in the grid and you can open it instantly. The detail view is where this app really earns its keep." |
| **[1:25–1:50]** Item Detail: large photo on the left, badges across the top (Type, Subtype, Location, Discontinued), two-column info grid on the right. | "Up top: type, subtype, where it lives in your craft room, and whether it's still in production. Below that: item number, theme, location, price, where you bought it, and any notes you want to keep." |
| **[1:50–2:10]** Click **Edit** next to Sentiments. Type a few sentiments separated by commas. Save. Pills appear, each with a tiny ✕ to remove. | "Every stamp set can carry a list of sentiments: the actual sayings printed on the stamps. Type them in once, and every word becomes searchable across your whole inventory." |
| **[2:10–2:30]** Scroll down to **Sentiments from this Set** card → small image snips appear next to each sentiment. | "If you've cropped sentiment images, they show up here next to the text: so you can scan visually instead of reading every line." |
| **[2:30–2:55]** Scroll to **Stock** section. Click **Edit** → adjust quantity inline → Save. Stock badge on the card updates. | "Stock tracking is built right in. Click Edit, adjust the quantity, and the running total updates everywhere: including your Home dashboard's 'Running low' panel." |
| **[2:55–3:15]** Scroll to **Purchase History** card. Fill in Qty, Price Per Item, Date → click Add. The new row appears with running total and total spend. | "Every time you restock, log it here. Quantity, price, date. Over time you build a complete spending history: feeding into the Purchase Report view." |
| **[3:15–3:25]** Pan to right sidebar: **Related Items** thumbnails and **Gallery** strip. Click a related item → it opens. | "On the right, related items link matching stamps, dies, and stencils together: so finding one finds them all." |
| **[3:25–3:30]** End card: "Next up: **Projects**." | "Next up, we'll turn these supplies into projects." |

## Shot list reminders

- Use a real, populated inventory. Empty grids don't sell the search demo.
- "Thank you" is the best search demo word, it shows hits across multiple item types.
- The Edit-Sentiments and Edit-Stock buttons are easy to miss: zoom in or callout-arrow them.
