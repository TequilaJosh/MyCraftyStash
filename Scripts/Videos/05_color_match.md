# 05 — Color Match

**Target runtime:** ~2:30
**Goal:** Show that Color Match turns a reference photo into a usable palette — matched to DMC floss numbers, OLO marker codes, and any cardstock you already own.

---

| VISUAL | VOICEOVER |
|---|---|
| **[0:00–0:10]** B-roll: a crafter holding their phone, scrolling Pinterest. They stop on a card image with a beautiful palette. | "You see a card you love. You want those exact colors. But which floss? Which marker? Do you already own the right cardstock?" |
| **[0:10–0:20]** Cut to MCS sidebar. Click **Color Match**. View opens with two tabs visible: **✦ DMC Floss** and **✎ OLO Marker.** | "Color Match takes the guesswork out. It lives in the sidebar, and it opens with two tabs — one for DMC floss, one for OLO markers." |
| **[0:20–0:40]** On the DMC Floss tab. Drag a reference image into the upload area. The image appears on the left. | "Drop in any reference image. A card you photographed, a Pinterest screenshot, a fabric swatch — anything with the colors you want." |
| **[0:40–1:05]** Cursor turns into an eyedropper. Click on a teal area of the image. A swatch appears on the right showing the picked color and its closest DMC floss match — number and name. | "Click anywhere on the image and the eyedropper picks the color. Instantly you get the nearest DMC floss — the actual thread number you'd buy at the store." |
| **[1:05–1:25]** Pick three more colors. Each one adds a row. Each row shows: picked color swatch, DMC number, and — if you own matching cardstock — a thumbnail of the inventory item next to it. | "Pick as many colors as you want. And here's where it gets clever — if you already own cardstock in a matching shade, the inventory item appears right next to the match. You'll know before you start whether you need to shop." |
| **[1:25–1:45]** Switch to the **OLO Marker** tab. The same image stays loaded. Pick colors → matches now show OLO marker codes (e.g., R128, BG52). | "Switch to the OLO Marker tab and the same picks now show OLO codes — the markers you actually have in your drawer." |
| **[1:45–2:05]** Wide shot of the full Color Match screen with a complete palette built out — image on left, four matches on right, with inventory thumbnails. | "Two minutes ago you had a photo. Now you have a palette: DMC numbers, OLO codes, and a check against your own stash." |
| **[2:05–2:25]** Quick montage: take a screenshot of the palette → drop it into a Project's notes → open the Card Build Wizard with the cardstock match already selected. | "Drop the palette into a project's notes and your build is half-planned before you've even cut paper." |
| **[2:25–2:30]** End card: "Next up — **Wish List**." | "Last stop — your wish list." |

---

## Shot list reminders

- Pre-load a reference image with at least 4 distinct colors so the eyedropper demo has range.
- Make sure inventory contains cardstock in at least one of the picked colors — that "match" reveal is the wow moment.
- The OLO tab transition reuses the image; emphasize that with a quick screen-wipe rather than a hard cut.
