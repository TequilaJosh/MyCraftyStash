# 07 — Advertisement: Feature Montage

**Target runtime:** ~0:55 (target 45–60s)
**Format:** Feature montage with upbeat music — fast cuts, big text callouts, voiceover light enough to mute and still follow.
**Use cases:** YouTube pre-roll, Instagram Reels / TikTok, app store hero video, embedded on the homepage.

---

## Music & pacing

- **Bed**: upbeat, warm acoustic / indie-pop. Around 110–120 BPM. Cut on the beat.
- **Sound design**: subtle UI clicks aligned to cursor moments. One "ding" when each big text callout lands.
- **Voiceover level**: -12 dB under the music. The VO is icing — the visuals carry the spot.

## Cut sheet (for the editor)

1. Desk B-roll (0:00) — 4 seconds, slight slow-mo, warm color grade.
2. Home dashboard (0:04) — full-resolution screen capture, stat numbers animate in.
3. Inventory search (0:08) — capture the live filter as you type "thank you". Trim to the moment results settle.
4. Item Detail montage (0:14) — three micro-clips: sentiment edit, stock +1, purchase row.
5. Card Build Wizard (0:20) — three button "Done!" state changes on beat. This is the most satisfying moment in the spot — give it a tiny breath.
6. Color Match (0:26) — eyedropper pick → DMC reveal → cardstock thumbnail glow.
7. Wish List (0:32) — tab switches keyed to music beats. TE import flashes by — don't dwell.
8. Project remake (0:38) — focus on the stock numbers ticking down. That's the magic moment.
9. Hero shot (0:44) — full app, sidebar nav glowing one-by-one.
10. End card (0:50) — clean logo lockup with URL. Hold for 5 full seconds — viewers need time to read.

---

## Variants

- **30-second cutdown** (for paid social): keep 0:00, 0:08, 0:20, 0:32, 0:44, 0:50. Skip Item Detail and Color Match.
- **15-second teaser** (for Reels / TikTok): open on the Card Build Wizard "Done!" sequence cold, no setup. End on logo at 0:13.
- **Silent autoplay version**: bump every on-screen text callout from 6pt body to ~14pt and hold each two beats longer.
